# Milestone 2 handoff: "Nape"

The first Titan is in. It wanders the district, notices you by sight or by the sound of your gas, chases, winds up and grabs. Hooks catch on its body and ride with it. E slashes, and a fast cut through the nape kills it: it collapses and steams, and a new one appears out of sight 12 s later. Blades wear, and orange rooftop crates refill gas and blades.

Everything in `docs/GOAL.md` is green except one spec number that can't be met as written (supply coverage, below). **Your feel review is next.** No M1 value was changed, `autoPull` is still 8, and nothing was tuned after the first build.

## How to play

`npm run dev`, then open http://127.0.0.1:5180 and click.

| Input | Action |
|---|---|
| Mouse, left and right click, Shift, Space, WASD | As in M1 |
| **E** | Slash. A cut through the nape at 15 m/s or more (relative to the Titan) with blades left kills; anything else makes it flinch |
| **Space (mash)** | Break free of a grab |
| R / Esc / F3 / F4 | Reset / pause / performance overlay / tuning panel (every new value is in F4) |

- **The HUD:** gas and blades at the bottom, and kills top right. A white hit marker means a cut connected. A big red one means a kill. "GRABBED" shows an escape bar. "RESUPPLYING" shows while you stand on a crate.
- **Supply crates:** signal orange, with a flag. The nearest one is on the roof just north of the spawn.

## What was built

- **The Titan in the sim core** (pure TypeScript, 120 Hz, deterministic):
  - **Body.** One pose function (`src/sim/titan-body.ts`) turns the Titan's state into part transforms, capsules and the nape. The sim uses them for hooks, collision, grabs and cuts; the renderer poses `titan.glb` from the same transforms, so what you see is what you hit.
  - **Navigation.** A 4 m grid, dilated by the largest Titan's footprint, with connected components and A* plus string pulling.
  - **Brain.** Wander, notice, chase, wind-up, grab, hold, recover, flinch, collapse, dead.
- **Grabs.** The wind-up aims at where you will be when it strikes. The hand catches you only if your body is inside it on the strike tick. Mash Space to escape; after 3 s it lets go anyway and you lose 10 gas. Either way you're thrown clear, with 2 s of grace.
- **Hooks on a Titan** are stored as a body part plus a local point and ride with the body. The cable clips your velocity relative to the anchor. A kill releases them.
- **Slash, blades, supply** as specified. One rule is interpreted, under "Values chosen or changed" below.
- **Assets.**
  - `tools/blender/titan.py` builds a 2,016-triangle block giant: big head, dark hair cap, lidless eyes and a flat stepped grin. It reads the skeleton straight from `titan-body.ts`.
  - The district now has 8 rooftop supply crates and 6 Titan spawn markers.
- **Render, UI, audio.**
  - **Titan:** its model, a steam column after a kill, and `slash` and `grabbed` poses for Levi.
  - **Camera:** footstep shake by distance, grab shake and zoom, a kill kick, and camera collision against the Titan.
  - **HUD and sound:** the HUD above, and synthesized sounds for every fight event.
- **Tests.** 18 fixture scenarios plus `titan-wander` in the district, with 36 new headless tests. The M1 tests are unchanged.

## Values chosen or changed

All spec starting values are in, unchanged: see [tuning-M2.md](tuning-M2.md) for the full table (112 constants). A few rules and values the spec left open:

| Value | Choice | Why |
|---|---|---|
| Wander pause, chase re-path, notice turn, flinch, collapse, hand close and arm return | 3 s, 0.3 s, 0.6 s, 0.8 s, 2.5 s, 0.25 s, 0.6 s | Timings the spec didn't give |
| Grab only within 60 degrees of facing | 60 | So it never grabs behind its back |
| Escape press decay | 1.5 presses/s | "Presses decay over time". Mashing at about 10 per second escapes in about 1 s |
| Footsteps felt out to | 90 m | The spec gives the strength at 20 m (0.25); it falls off as 20/distance and fades to zero by 90 m |
| Grab shake / zoom / kill kick | 0.5 / 8 deg / 0.8 | Camera values the spec named but didn't size |
| Respawn out of view | Outside 60 degrees of your look, or behind a building | "Out of the player's view" |
| **Slash window rule** | A slash whose window passes through the nape at any tick is a nape hit; a body hit resolves only when the window ends | The nape sits right above the upper back. Tested tick by tick, the blade touched the back one tick before the nape, so an approach from behind flinched instead of killing. The rule now matches "nape first, then the other capsules" over the whole swing |
| Nape position | 0.745 x height, just behind the neck and above the torso | The first placement was buried in the top of the torso capsule |

**One spec number can't be met: supply coverage.** The spec asks for "six to eight crates, one within about 150 m of anywhere inside the Wall." That isn't geometrically possible: covering a 1.5 km square to within 150 m needs about 38 points. With 8 crates spread by a minimax search, the worst spot in the district is **394 m** from a crate. The asset test checks that measured value, so any drift would show. **Your call:** keep 8 crates (resupply is a real trip), or allow about 20 (a crate within about 250 m). The crate near the spawn is 17.5 m away.

## The three weakest things about the fight (honest)

1. **Hitting the nape will probably be frustrating at first, not fun.** The nape is a 1 m target tucked behind the neck, just above the upper back. You have to arrive at 15 m/s relative, from behind, at shoulder height, and press E within about 0.25 s of arrival. Even the scripted pilot needed its start height searched: from 2 m lower, its swing sagged into the lower back and only drew a flinch. M1 was already hard to control. This adds a moving, turning target, and the one skill that matters has the least forgiveness. What does work: a missed cut is always readable (a flinch, a clang and a white marker), and simply swinging past the nape with the reel lands near the kill speed. The no-reel approach cut at 12.9 m/s, so "fast enough" is not the hard part; *position* is.
2. **Grabs are binary and a bit predictable.** The wind-up locks its aim at the start and the hand only catches you on one tick. Anything moving across the arm at a few m/s dodges trivially. Hanging still near its shoulders is always caught. A grab can also throw you straight into a wall: on `grab-ledge`, the throw pinned Levi to the block. A good grab should read as "I got careless", but right now it reads as "I stood still".
3. **The Titan is a street-bound, stiff walker.** Its path grid is dilated for the largest Titan, so it only uses avenues, wide streets, squares and the ring road; narrow lanes are walls to it. It turns in place, and it can't crouch or reach up onto roofs. So anything on a roof over about 14 m, or in a narrow lane, is completely safe, and on the ground right at its feet you're safe too. Its gait is readable, but it has no weight shift or stumble, and the collapse is a single rigid topple.

Also worth knowing: the Titan only notices within 70 m, so in the district it often ignores you until you're close. And the 8 supply crates are far apart (above).

## Proposed assist values (not applied)

To try in F4 if the nape is frustrating. None of these are applied; each needs your OK through the feel gate:

| Proposal | From | To | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| Blade reach (`bladeReach`) | 2.2 m | 3.0 m | A bigger cut volume everywhere. The simplest way to make the nape easier to catch |
| Nape kill speed (`napeKillSpeed`) | 15 m/s | 12 m/s | A plain swing past the nape kills; reeling stops being required |
| Aim-assist cone (`aimAssistCone`) | 3 deg | 5 deg | Hooks snap to a moving Titan's body more easily. This is an M1 value, so it also affects buildings |
| Grab wind-up (`grabWindup`) | 0.7 s | 0.9 s | More time to react to the raised arm |
| Notice range (`noticeRange`) | 70 m | 55 m | More room to set up an approach from behind |
| New: nape reach bonus | none | +1.0 m on the blade sphere when testing the nape only | Makes the nape easier to hit without making body hits easier. It needs a small code change, so it's not in F4 yet |

## Things to try

1. Start in `?play=titan-yard`. Hook the Titan's back (the crosshair turns teal on its body), reel in, and press E as you pass behind the neck. Try again without reeling to feel the difference.
2. Stand still on the roof edge in `?play=grab-ledge`, get grabbed, and mash Space. Then try dodging with gas the moment its arm goes up.
3. From the spawn roof in the default game, look for the Titan down the avenue. Get its attention with a gas burst within 35 m while out of sight, then break line of sight for 8 s and watch it give up.
4. Swing off the rooftops and land on its shoulders. Watch its head track you.
5. Burn gas and blades, then land on the orange crate next to the spawn.
6. In F4, try the proposed assists above and export a before and after table for anything you like.

## Known gaps and notes

- **The browser runner is headless now.** It never opens a window on the desktop, and the adapter check still confirms the real Apple GPU (`apple metal-3`). Set `ODM_HEADED=1` to watch.
- **Live-play checks and pointer lock.** Chrome refuses pointer lock when its window isn't the active one on the desktop, which is the case for the automated browser while the laptop is in use. The features runner therefore plays with `?nolock=1`: the game behaves as if the pointer were locked, while the keys and mouse clicks are still real. Pointer lock itself works when you click to play.
- **Steam** is instanced, translucent spheres: readable, but greybox.
- **Perf "Titan in view."** The perf flight orbits the Titan at 90 m and 38 m up, looking at it, and records the share of frames with the Titan on screen. The M1 flight is kept as `?scenario=perf-m1`.
